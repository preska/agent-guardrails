---
id: DEC-002
title: Money is Decimal, never float
level: error
---

## Decision

Amounts in billing are `decimal.Decimal` from end to end.

## Why

Binary floats can't represent most cents exactly: `0.1 + 0.2` is
`0.30000000000000004`. Totals drift by a cent and reconciliation fails.

## Rules

```rule
forbid call float
in examples/billing/**
```

## Fix

Use `Decimal(str_value)` and round with `quantize(CENT, rounding=ROUND_HALF_UP)`,
as `examples/billing/invoice.py` does.
