---
id: DEC-003
title: Log with the logger, not print
level: warning
---

## Decision

Services write diagnostics through `logging`, never `print()`.

## Why

`print` output has no level, no timestamp and no request id, and it vanishes
when the service runs under a process manager.

## Rules

```rule
forbid call print
in examples/**
```

## Fix

`log = logging.getLogger(__name__)` at the top of the module, then
`log.info(...)`.
